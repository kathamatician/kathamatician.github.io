About

Meihui Huang 黄美慧

Meihui Huang is a physicist based in Witten, where she helps turn promising setups into dependable measurements: supporting detector tests, analysis, and the small practical details that make an experiment behave. Away from the bench, she runs the social channels for maxclerkwell.tech with Stephan Bökelmann (MaxClerkwell). Equal parts careful observer and cheerful keeper of the signal, she likes work that makes both instruments and ideas easier to read.

maxclerkwell.tech is Stephan’s site; Meihui is its collaborator, not its namesake. Research is present here as a compact record, while the day-to-day centre of gravity remains measurement and communication.
